First VR visit in Brisbane: what to expect

Know what to expect before your first visit: what to wear, game ages and how booking works.

Meta3 VR is a free-roam arena in Newstead. You walk the real floor with a headset on and a blaster in your hands, and the game world moves with you. There are no pods: your squad shares one open space.

What to wear

Wear comfortable clothes and closed shoes you can move in. You will walk, turn and duck, so skip anything long or loose that could catch.

  • Closed shoes
  • Clothes you can move freely in

Ready for round one?

Arena games from $39 a player, Wednesday to Friday. Regular price $59.

Book now

How a session runs

Check your booking for game length and arrival instructions. Your squad then plays together in the same space.

Which game to pick

Pick by mood. Overrun is zombie survival. Legend of Faragor is a fantasy adventure. See every game on the experiences page.

Questions

How old do you need to be?

Age limits vary by game. Toon Strike, Legend of Faragor and Escapage are 8+. Overrun, Virtual Arena and the zombie games are 10+.

How many people can play at once?

Up to 10 players per session.

Can we bring food?

Parties can bring their own food and soft drinks. No alcohol.
