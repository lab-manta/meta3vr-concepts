Questions

Before you book

How old do players need to be?

Games start at age 8. Toon Strike, Legend of Faragor and Escapage are 8+. Overrun, Virtual Arena and the zombie games are 10+.

How many people can play at once?

1 to 10 players per game. For bigger groups, book a private event.

How long is a game?

Each game runs 30 minutes.

Can we bring food?

Parties and private events can bring their own food and soft drinks. No alcohol.

Is the venue accessible?

Google lists a wheelchair accessible entrance, toilet and parking. Call us to talk through any needs.
